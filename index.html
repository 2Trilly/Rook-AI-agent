Make it genuinely useful

In Apple Shortcuts, create a shortcut for a task you repeat, then use its Share Link and add it in Settings. ROOK will show your shortcut as a button. Review the shortcut before running it, especially if it sends messages, shares location, or changes data.

What this build can and cannot do
  • Works as a local-first PWA with memory, notes, shortcut links, and browser searches.
  • AI responses require a compatible provider and your own configuration.
  • Live research opens a search engine; prices and product details must be checked at the source.
  • iOS limits app-to-app actions. This app cannot silently control every app or send messages on your behalf.
